Refund Policy

Every file is prepared and checked by the seller himself before it is delivered. If something still goes wrong on our side, you are protected:

  • Not delivered: if your file is not delivered within the delivery time promised on the product page, you can ask for a full refund of what you paid.
  • Wrong file: if the delivered file is wrong, or has a mistake we cannot fix, you get a full refund. Mistakes are always fixed free first — message us and the corrected file is re-uploaded in the app.
  • Custom orders: if we cannot make what was agreed with you, you get a full refund. A preview is shown before final delivery on custom work.
  • Correctly delivered files: because digital files can be copied, a file that was delivered correctly cannot be returned, so change-of-mind refunds are not possible after delivery. Please use the FREE Sample Pack to check the quality before you buy.
  • How to ask: message on WhatsApp or email shakircadet1@gmail.com with your Order ID. Approved refunds are sent back by Easypaisa within 3 working days.

  • Refunds follow the Refund Policy above.

Privacy Policy
